# Example prompts

Paste any of these into Cursor (Agent) after the `postfast` server shows a green dot in Customize, then MCPs. Nothing is published: the assistant creates drafts and you press Post in PostFast.

1. `Which channels are connected on my PostFast account?`
2. `Draft a YouTube Short from https://example.com/clip.mp4. Ask me for the title, privacy and made-for-kids before you create the draft.`
3. `Draft this video on my TikTok channel. Ask me for who can view it, comments, duet, stitch and commercial-content disclosure first.`
4. `List my PostFast posts from 2026-11-01 to 2026-11-15 and show each state as returned.`
5. `Which of my posts are still drafts?`
